# D35 Statblock Converter 0.4.1

This patch incorporates findings from the first live Roll20 import of the
authoritative DOCX workflow.

- Relies on the D&D 3.5 sheet's Initiative token action instead of creating a
  duplicate custom action.
- Ignores empty structured JSON values so they cannot appear as
  `[object Object]` in sheet text.
- Preserves parsed class names and levels in Roll20 JSON.
- Routes full Sneak Attack and Trapfinding entries to Class Abilities for
  creatures with rogue levels, without duplicating their abbreviated upper
  summaries or placing them in Racial Abilities.
- Emits each feat once, in source-list order, using its full lower entry when
  available instead of prepending a consolidated feat-name list.
- Supports `Possessions combat gear, ...` variants without creating a bogus
  `combat gear` item, and keeps expanded potion/tool rules in Other rather than
  Racial Abilities.
- Routes standard barbarian features such as Rage and Fast Movement to Class
  Abilities and recognizes combined masterwork equipment reference entries.
- Distinguishes bracketed skill tricks from feat categories and uses an untyped
  lower entry as full feat text when its name matches the upper Feats list.
- Preserves numeric thousands separators inside possession descriptions instead
  of splitting values such as `1,000 gp` into separate items.
- Routes owned-item property entries such as Cloak of Resistance and Keen to
  Other, while retaining class-feature precedence for overlapping names.
- Keeps bold temporary stat lines attached to their potion entry and routes
  standard swashbuckler features to Class Abilities.
- Routes campaign affiliation entries to Other rather than Racial Abilities.
- Suppresses feat-derived inline summaries such as `Dodge +1` when the feat is
  already represented in the Feats field, allowing class-and-item-only NPCs to
  retain an empty Racial Abilities field.
- Keeps comma-separated artisan-tool specialties together as one possession,
  removes duplicated Listen/Spot skill totals from biography text, and keeps
  expanded dwarf traits as one Racial Abilities entry without class-summary
  duplicates.
- Supports `male or female` identity prefixes, semicolon-separated domain-slot
  spells with trailing `D` markers, and intentional spontaneous-cure reference
  entries without false missing-spell warnings. Cleric aura, domain,
  spellcasting, spontaneous-casting, and turning rules now route to Class
  Abilities, while domain footnotes stay out of biography text.
- Suppresses abbreviated equipment-derived attack options such as `set spear
  against charge` when the full owned-item rule is already preserved in Other.
- Also suppresses generic equipment-derived attack options such as `trip` when
  the full owned-weapon rule describes that option in Other.
- Preserves prepared-spell level boundaries represented by hard line breaks,
  filters wizard bonus-feat footnotes from Biography, and keeps affiliation
  summaries out of Biography when the full affiliation entry is in Other.
- Recognizes lower feat headings whose name and bracketed category are split
  across adjacent bold runs without a separating space, and normalizes their
  full Roll20 feat text.
- Matches equipment headings with suffix-form enhancement bonuses to possession
  entries with prefix-form bonuses, such as `Heavy Steel Shield +1` and
  `+1 heavy steel shield`.
- Preserves heading-like save labels inside parentheses, such as
  `stench (Fort DC 16)`, and filters standalone Listen/Spot sense totals from
  Biography even when those skills are absent from the upper Skills line.
