Dodge [General] The expanded feat monster gains a +1 dodge bonus to Armor Class against one designated opponent.
Power Attack [General] The expanded feat monster may trade melee attack bonus for melee damage.

No fixed Power Attack value is included because the amount is chosen separately for each attack.
Improved Initiative [General] The expanded feat monster gains a +4 bonus on initiative checks.
Ferocity (Ex) The expanded feat monster continues fighting without penalty while disabled or dying.
Skills The listed modifiers already include applicable conditional rules. Diplomacy +3 includes the +2 synergy bonus from Sense Motive.
