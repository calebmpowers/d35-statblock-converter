Acrobatic Backstab [Movement Skill Trick] Once per encounter, a successful Tumble check can leave the target flat-footed against the pirate's next melee attack.
Blur The pirate carries a potion of blur that grants concealment for 3 minutes.
Cloak of Resistance The pirate's cloak grants a +1 resistance bonus on saving throws.
Improved Toughness [General] The pirate gains 1 hit point per Hit Die.
Keen The pirate's +1 keen rapier has an expanded threat range.
Sneak Attack (Ex) The pirate deals an extra 1d6 points of damage when it flanks an opponent or when the opponent is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC.
Trapfinding (Ex) The pirate can use Search to locate traps with a DC higher than 20 and can use Disable Device to disarm magic traps.
Two-Weapon Fighting The pirate reduces its two-weapon attack penalties while using a light off-hand weapon.
